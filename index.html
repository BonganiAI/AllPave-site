Good to know

Questions we get asked

How does the free quote work? +

We come out, measure the area, talk through paver options and colours, and give you a written quote — at no cost and no obligation to go ahead.

How long does a typical job take? +

It depends on the size and ground conditions, but most driveways and patios are completed within a few days. We'll give you a clear timeline with your quote.

Can you match existing paving? +

In most cases, yes. Bring a photo or a sample paver and we'll do our best to match the style and colour for an extension or repair.

Which areas do you service? +

We service Pretoria and the greater Gauteng area. Send us your address on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.
